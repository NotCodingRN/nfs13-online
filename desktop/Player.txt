NFS13 Player for Windows

Extract the ZIP and open NFS13Player.exe. Each player uses their own copy.

1. Select the folder containing your own NFS13.exe.
2. Enter the display name your host expects (1–15 letters, numbers, _ or -).
3. Check the server IPv4 address (prefilled in host-created ZIPs), or change it.
4. Confirm that you own the game when prompted. Approve the Windows hosts-file
   update if asked, then choose online play in the game.

The launcher prepares NFS13.online.exe beside the original game and puts a
personal login ticket into its Blaze and Autolog requests. Multiple players
can launch simultaneously on the same Wi-Fi. Identity follows the launcher's
saved key, so a changed IP does not change your persona.

Keep a backup of %LOCALAPPDATA%\Fairhaven\identities.json to keep your identity
when moving to another PC. It contains your personal access key; do not share
it with other players. The server's player-data backup preserves persona IDs,
credential hashes and reported online XP. Local game saves stay on your PC.

This ticket patch supports the checked retail 1.5 game build. An unsupported
executable fails before launch instead of falling back to IP identification.
